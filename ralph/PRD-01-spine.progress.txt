WHAT PRD-09 (`/root/ezpug`, `ralph/PRD-09-iron-platform.md`) CONSUMES:
- The published package, pinned exactly (0.x: a minor is the breaking bump).
- `@ezpug/match-api/fake` — `createFakeOrchestrator({clock, ...})` plays a Bo1 in
  milliseconds under a fake clock and is what the platform's tests and its history seed run
  on. Its fault knobs (allocation refused, boot that never ends, crash with/without backup,
  duplicate and out-of-order deliveries, provider down, webhook endpoint failing N times)
  are the platform's failure-path tests.
- `@ezpug/match-api/fixtures` — the eleven flows and the recorded goldens; the platform runs
  the suite against the fake it pins, and a disagreement between the three implementations
  is decided by the fixture.
- `@ezpug/match-api/client` and `/webhooks` — the typed client (idempotency, the retry
  table, `subscribeStream`) and `verifyWebhook` + `createDeliveryDeduper`.
- `docs/match-api.md` (read this instead of our code) and `docs/gamemodes.md` (the manifest
  and the `WidgetHostMessage` handshake PRD-09 T7 implements as written).
